# Example 3: The conclusion mirrors the introduction

This example applies [06 — The conclusion closes the loop and opens](../Writing/06-Conclusion.md) from the [main guide](../README.md). Each row puts a sentence of the introduction next to the sentence of the Discussion that answers it. All sentences are as published.

## Self-aligned PDCS

Grégory Moille *et al.*, [*Self-aligned optical microcomb emerging between octave separated lasers*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11086-2), Nature **658**, 350–357 (2026).

### The problem, answered in its words ([Rule 1](../Writing/06-Conclusion.md#rules))

| Introduction | Discussion |
|---|---|
| `However, while DKS combs have proven their effectiveness in numerous applications, their outward cascading from a low-power seed pump laser leads to fundamental metrology limitations: Octave-spaced signals at the comb edges are typically very weak and noisy (due to thermo-refractive fluctuations), which critically hinders the detection and stabilization of the CEO.` | `Our self-aligned PDCS microcomb operates significantly differently from standard DKS microcombs that rely on outward cascading from a centred single pump laser, which results in weak and noisy signals at the comb edges, thus complicating the CEO detection and locking required for precision metrology.` |

### The claims, now demonstrated ([Rule 1](../Writing/06-Conclusion.md#rules))

| Introduction | Discussion |
|---|---|
| `We show that by simply changing how the input lasers are locked, the same self-aligned PDCS can perform the three core tasks of self-referenced OFCs [...]` | `We demonstrate the benefit of this versatility by performing three key tasks for integrated OFC systems [...]` |

### What the work opens ([Rule 6](../Writing/06-Conclusion.md#rules))

| Introduction | Discussion |
|---|---|
| `Crucially, because the octave-separated pumps are both comb lines (when operating in the self-alignment regime), the CEO of the subsequently generated PDCS is \textit{a priori} determined before the chip from the harmonic offset between the pumps, enabling flexible interfacing with microwave and optical frequency references.` | `Moving forward, our self-aligned PDCS enables several integration capabilities, many arising from the stabilized, octave-separated pumps that remain available for further use.` |

## Parametrically driven cavity solitons

Grégory Moille *et al.*, *Parametrically driven pure-Kerr temporal solitons in a chip-integrated microcavity*, Nature Photonics (2024).

### The question, answered ([Rule 1](../Writing/06-Conclusion.md#rules))

| Introduction | Discussion |
|---|---|
| `A natural question that arises is: is it possible to generate PDCSs in foundry-ready, pure-Kerr resonators with bichromatic driving?` | `We have shown theoretically, numerically, and experimentally that dispersive resonators with a purely Kerr-type $\chi^{(3)}$ nonlinearity can support parametrically driven cavity solitons under conditions of bichromatic driving.` |

### Each limitation, removed ([Rule 2](../Writing/06-Conclusion.md#rules))

| Introduction | Discussion |
|---|---|
| `noise on the external CW laser can degrade the coherence of nearby comb lines` | `[...] could result in fundamental advantages to noise characteristics.` |
| `removal of the CW background may require careful spectral filtering` | `Moreover, the lack of a dominating CW component at the PDCS carrier frequency alleviates the need for careful spectral shaping [...]` |
| `applications that require coexistence of distinguishable binary elements are fundamentally beyond reach.` | `Finally, the fact that PDCSs come in two forms with opposite phase opens the doors to a new range of applications that require a binary degree of freedom, including all-optical random number generation and realisations of coherent optical Ising machines.` |
